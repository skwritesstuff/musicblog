Vintage Blog Era Digital Archive

A meticulously preserved digital retrospective of The MuSiK Box and In Audio We Trust (2009–2012), originally created & curated by Seamus Kelleher and Myles Snider. Chronicling the golden era of indie hip-hop, mixtape culture, blog-house, and early co-signs for future icons.

⚠️ HISTORICAL ARCHIVAL NOTICE // INCOMPLETE SALVAGE

This site is an incomplete historical salvage of what was successfully recovered from fragmented Wayback Machine web snapshots. During its active run (2009–2012), The MuSiK Box and In Audio We Trust published multiple times every single day—covering thousands of underground mixtapes, MP3 premieres, and concert reviews. The 360 articles cataloged here represent only the small fraction that web crawlers happened to cache. Thousands of daily posts and media assets were never archived and remain lost to digital history.
